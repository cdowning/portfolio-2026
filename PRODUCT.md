# Product

## Platform

Static web portfolio, generated by Nuxt 4.

## Users and purpose

Prospective employers and hiring managers need a fast, credible understanding of Caitlin Hawley’s frontend engineering experience, design judgment, and fit. Peers and collaborators may explore her work or connect.

## Product contract

- Two public routes: `/` and `/resume`.
- Preserve verified employment history, contact details, and outbound destinations.
- Lead with the combination of frontend engineering and design craft.
- Use real public evidence; never fabricate testimonials, metrics, clients, or outcomes.
- Support keyboard use, visible focus, reduced motion, readable contrast, and responsive layouts.
- V1 is light-only; dark mode, CMS, contact forms, and hosting migration are out of scope.
