# Colors

The portfolio uses five approved primitive families and maps them to semantic roles for light and dark themes. Components normally consume semantic roles such as `--canvas`, `--text`, and `--action`; direct primitive use is reserved for approved authored swatches and drafting details.

Scale numbers are ordinal positions from `00` (lightest approved member) to `100` (darkest approved member). They are intentionally sparse and are not literal OKLCH lightness percentages. Hex values are canonical; measured OKLCH lightness orders the families and guides future additions.

## Extending the palette

Resolve a new color need in this order:

1. Use an existing semantic role when its purpose fits.
2. Otherwise use an approved primitive step.
3. If the closest family lacks the needed lightness, shape a candidate from that family in OKLCH.
4. Test foreground/background and adjacent-color contrast, then visually review every relevant theme.
5. Promote the accepted value together into CSS, `DESIGN.md`, and Storybook.

Generated Impeccable ramps remain exploratory until they complete this review. Unused or design-only values are not part of the public implemented palette.
